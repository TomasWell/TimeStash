## 3. 描述（中文）

```
【提前完成，省下的时间存起来】

时间收纳箱把「预估」当预算、「实际」当支出：提前做完的事，省下的时间会真的存进你的箱子。

• 开始一件事，定个大概要多久
• 计时中随时暂停、继续
• 提前完成 → 省下的时间立刻入箱，箱子肉眼可见地满起来
• 超时了也会照实记录，下次预估更准

【时间账本】
每一笔入箱都记着：哪件事、哪个分类、省了多少时间。按分类筛选、按周看趋势。

【越用越准的预估】
同一个分类攒够样本后，App 会给出「你平时估得太保守/太乐观」的提醒，
下次定预估就更有把握。

【适合谁】
• 总是拖到最后一刻才开始的人
• 想养成「先开始，再调整」习惯的人
• 需要对时间有掌控感的人

【隐私】
• 数据只存在你的设备上
• 不做自动网络请求，唯一联网是你手动点开外部链接
• 不需要注册账号，不上传任何内容

免费使用，无广告。
```

### 描述（英文）

```
【Save the time you finish early】

TimeStash treats your estimate like a budget and the real duration like spending.
Finish a task ahead of schedule and the saved minutes go straight into your box.

• Start a task with a rough estimate
• Pause and resume anytime
• Finish early → saved time is banked instantly, and the box visibly fills up
• Overruns are recorded too, so the next estimate gets sharper

【Your time ledger】
Every deposit is recorded: which task, which category, how much time saved.
Filter by category, follow the trend week by week.

【Estimates that improve with use】
Once a category has enough samples, TimeStash tells you whether you tend to
over- or under-estimate — so the next estimate is a better one.

【Who it's for】
• People who always start at the last minute
• Anyone building a "just start, then adjust" habit
• Anyone who wants a real sense of control over their time

【Privacy】
• Your data stays on your device
• No automatic network requests; the only network use is links you tap yourself
• No account needed, nothing uploaded

Free, no ads.
```
